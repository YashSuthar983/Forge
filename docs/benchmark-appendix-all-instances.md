# Appendix: every instance, every number

Complete per-instance results. Nothing is summarised away: every cell is read
back out of the raw `.jsonl` a run wrote, and no number here is recomputed from
a summary table. Aggregates and interpretation live in
[`benchmark-campaign-2026-09.md`](benchmark-campaign-2026-09.md) and
[`benchmarks.md`](benchmarks.md); this page is the evidence under them.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/gen_benchmark_appendix.py`.

## How to read this

The numbers come from **five runs on three machines**. They are not
interchangeable, and no table below mixes two of them in one row group.

| # | run | machine | protocol | solvers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | sequential campaign, 28–29 Sep 2026 | Ryzen 7 7700X, 8c/16t, 30 GB | 60 s and 300 s, 8 threads and 1 thread, one solve at a time | Forge, HiGHS 1.15.1, SCIP 10.0 |
| **B** | 7-column Netlib run, 4 Sep 2026 | `yash-Bravo-15-B5DD`, 12 logical cores | 30 s, sequential per instance | 3 Forge engines, HiGHS, CBC, SciPy ×2 |
| **C** | MIPLIB-easy run, 5 Sep 2026 | same laptop as B | 30 s | Forge, HiGHS |
| **D** | QPLIB sweep, 24 Sep 2026 | `shreyas-radeon` | 60 s, 8 threads, **4 solves concurrent** | Forge only, plus an independent point check |
| **E** | GPU-parallel campaign, 9–10 Oct 2026 | Ryzen 7 7700X, 8c/16t, 30 GB, RX 9060 XT | 120 s (QPLIB) / 300 s (NETLIB/MIPLIB), 14 workers parallel | Forge `docs/benchmark-campaign` Release+native+Vulkan, CPU and GPU backends |

Run **D** is superseded by **A** for timing, because four concurrent solves on
eight cores measure the machine under contention rather than the solver. It is
kept here for one reason: it is the run whose every returned point was
re-evaluated by `scripts/qplib_eval.py`, so it carries the
independent-verification columns.

Conventions:

- `—` means no value was recorded. It never means zero.
- Objectives are printed to 6 significant figures; the raw files hold full
  precision.
- A **bold** objective means that solver *proved* optimality on that instance.
  An unbolded number is a feasible point with an open gap, however good it looks.
- Times are wall clock in seconds, measured outside the solver process, so they
  include model read and build, not just the solve.
